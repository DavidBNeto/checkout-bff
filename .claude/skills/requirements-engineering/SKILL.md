# Requirements Engineering

## Workflow
1. Identify stakeholders, actors, goals, system boundary and scope.
2. Separate functional requirements, non-functional requirements, constraints and business rules.
3. Phrase each requirement as one testable obligation with a unique ID.
4. Capture rationale, source, priority, dependencies and acceptance evidence.
5. Identify ambiguity, contradiction, missing states and exceptional paths.
6. Keep assumptions and open questions in separate sections.

## Quality criteria
A good requirement is necessary, unambiguous, atomic, feasible, verifiable, traceable and implementation-neutral where possible.

Avoid vague terms such as fast, user-friendly, robust or secure unless measurable criteria are supplied.

## Suggested format
- ID and title
- Statement ("The system shall...")
- Rationale/source
- Priority
- Preconditions and trigger
- Expected outcome
- Exceptions and constraints
- Acceptance criteria
- Related requirements and artifacts

Do not invent business policy. Ask for clarification or label a proposal explicitly.
